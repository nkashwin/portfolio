01 / THE BRIEF

How do you make an ordinary bag extraordinary?

THE CHALLENGE

Grocery delivery bags are functional, disposable and easily forgotten. The brief was to make Dunzo Daily’s packaging stand out, create brand recall and give customers something worth talking about.

STAND OUTBUILD RECALLSPARK CONVERSATION
02 / THE IDEA

What if delivery bags could talk like our favourite movies?

Groceries meet Indian cinema. Familiar movie references, playful illustrations and clever wordplay reimagined everyday grocery bags as little pieces of pop culture. My contribution was to the creative packaging design as part of the Dunzo team.

03 / THE EXECUTION #BagonMeinLelo

Three bags. Three iconic movies.

Movie-inspired copy, quirky characters and Dunzo’s distinctive green identity brought the idea to life.

04 / THE IMPACT

CUSTOMERS SHARED THE BAGS.

Customers photographed their deliveries, shared the jokes online and even found new uses for the packaging. The original posts below show how an everyday delivery became a memorable brand moment.

DUNZO / PROJECT 08 / APP REVAMP

A fresh app
experience.

Refreshing the everyday Dunzo experience through a more cohesive visual language, from category listings to the smallest icons.

MY CONTRIBUTION
Visual design · In-app graphics · Iconography · Category creatives

01 / THE CHALLENGE

Familiar app.
Fresher perspective.

The existing experience had evolved across different visual treatments. The revamp brought those elements into a more unified, brand-aligned system while keeping the everyday experience recognisable.

02 / ICONOGRAPHY

Small details.
Consistent language.

A refreshed family of icons brought a shared visual style to services and supporting interface elements. The goal was to keep the graphics simple, recognisable and consistent across the app.

03 / CATEGORY LISTINGS

Colour with
a purpose.

Category creatives used distinct colours and illustrated cues to give each service its own character, while retaining a coherent visual system across the listing experience.

THE TAKEAWAY

One app.
One visual language.

From icons to category creatives, the project demonstrates how thoughtful visual design can reinforce a familiar brand throughout an everyday digital experience.
